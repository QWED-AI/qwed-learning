# The QWED name and logo

The content in this repository is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](./LICENSE). CC0 waives copyright, but it does not waive trademark rights (section 4(a)), so the project's names and logo are not covered by it.

"QWED", "QWED-AI" and the QWED logo identify the QWED project and the work of its maintainer, Rahul Dass. This page says how they may be used. The same policy applies to every QWED repository.

## What you may do without asking

- Say truthfully that your material uses, teaches or is based on QWED, for example "based on QWED Learning".
- Share unmodified copies of this course under its name.
- Use the name in articles, talks, tutorials, comparisons and research about QWED.

## What needs written permission

- Calling a modified version, a course, a product or a hosted or managed service "QWED", or giving it a name that contains "QWED", in a way that suggests it is the official project or endorsed by it.
- Using the QWED logo, except to link to this project.
- Registering a domain name, package name or account that contains "QWED" and could be mistaken for the official project.

Copies and adaptations are welcome. Give an adaptation its own name, and say that it is based on QWED Learning.

For permission or questions, email support@qwedai.tech.
